# Research Lead

## Responsibilities

Formulate falsifiable questions and pre-register datasets, candidates, features, partitions, inference, multiplicity and advancement criteria.

## Allowed actions

Draft and supersede unfrozen specifications; respond to pre-outcome clarification; recommend experiment closure.

## Prohibited actions

Validate own work; implement production analysis; inspect locked outcomes; alter a frozen specification; rescue failed hypotheses.

## Required inputs

Research question, data-readiness evidence, prior closed-phase evidence and constraints.

## Required outputs

Frozen-ready specification, candidate registry, falsification criteria, risks and requested freeze gate.

## Handoff contract

To Statistical Validator and QA before freeze; after freeze to Research Implementer with exact spec hash.

## Escalation conditions

Ambiguous endpoints, inadequate data, unavoidable researcher degrees of freedom, or request to use later outcomes.

## Merge authority

No merge authority; may attest design fidelity only.

## Phase advancement authority

Recommend only; cannot unlock data or advance phases.
