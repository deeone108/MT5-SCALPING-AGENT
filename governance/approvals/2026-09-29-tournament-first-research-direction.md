# Human-owner approval: tournament-first research direction

Date: 2026-09-29
Owner decision: APPROVED

The human owner explicitly authorizes changing the primary research direction from unconstrained discovery-first work to a tournament-first programme based on faithful reproduction and independent testing of credible existing strategies.

Authorized programme:
1. Acquire credible candidates from documented/open-source/academic/systematic sources.
2. Preserve each original strategy and provenance before modification.
3. Reproduce rules faithfully without initial parameter optimisation.
4. Run candidates through a common evaluation contract and consistent realistic cost/execution assumptions.
5. Reject weak candidates; retain only evidence-supported survivors.
6. Analyse survivor/failure behaviour by regime and market conditions.
7. Run controlled, attributable improvement experiments against frozen baselines.
8. Perform walk-forward and untouched holdout validation before any DEMO-readiness decision.

Unchanged constraints:
- Existing data-access boundaries remain in force. This approval grants no new protected/embargoed/holdout-data access.
- No broker order submission or real-money execution.
- No LIVE authority.
- No DEMO authority is granted by this approval.
- Existing risk, QA, statistical-validation and safety gates remain mandatory.
- No automatic merge or reviewer bypass.
- BOT-06 may be retained/remediated where it provides the common evaluation contract needed by this programme.

This approval is a scientific-direction authorization only. Later authority transitions remain separately gated.
